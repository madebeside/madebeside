A few things
worth talking through.

Where do you work?+

Made Beside is based in Toronto and works with businesses across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area (GTA) on social media management.

Which platforms do you manage?+

We select the platforms together based on your audience and business. The channels included are confirmed in the project scope; we do not assume every brand needs to be everywhere.

What do you need before starting?+

Useful starting points include your existing social profiles, brand guidance, available assets and business priorities. We agree a secure way to grant account access. Do not send passwords through the inquiry form.

Who approves the content?+

We agree an approval process with your team before publishing. Let us know who needs to review content and which subjects need extra care.

How often will you publish or respond?+

Posting cadence, reporting frequency and comment or DM coverage depend on the agreed scope. We do not promise round-the-clock coverage or a fixed schedule before that conversation.

Does this include paid advertising?+

Paid-ad buying and account management are not part of this offer. Made Beside can provide digital marketing strategy and campaign creative; media buying needs to be handled separately.

What happens to the content and accounts?+

Asset delivery, access, usage rights and handover are confirmed in your agreement. We make responsibilities clear before the work begins.
