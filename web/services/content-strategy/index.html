A few questions.

Do we need to know exactly what we want?

No. Bring the business context and the challenge you are trying to solve. We can work through the content questions together.

What should we prepare?

Existing brand guidance, social profiles, website content, past campaigns and customer insights are useful. Share what is available; we will agree what else is needed during scoping.

Does strategy include content production?

Strategy and production are scoped explicitly. We can discuss photography, videography and social creative alongside the plan, but they are not assumed to be included in every strategy project.

How long does the process take?

Timing depends on the scope, available information and feedback process. We agree a schedule before work begins rather than promising a standard turnaround for every business.

How is the strategy delivered?

We agree the deliverables and format for your team at the start, then walk through the direction and next steps together.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. The process is collaborative. Tell us who handles content now and where an outside creative perspective would help.
