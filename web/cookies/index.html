Cookie policy

No advertising cookies. No visitor analytics added to this website.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Loading animation preference

A session-storage flag remembers that the opening animation has been shown in this tab, so it does not repeat as you navigate. It contains no contact details and is not used for analytics or advertising.

What this application uses

The website application does not set cookies or use local storage, advertising pixels, session replay, browser fingerprinting or visitor analytics. Fonts and visual elements are served with the site. There are no embedded social feeds, maps or video players that load third-party tracking content. The motion preference exists only in the current page and resets when it is reloaded.

Hosting and access services

Hosting and access providers may use necessary security, routing or sign-in technologies and maintain technical logs. A private site preview may require provider authentication. Those services are separate from optional tracking configured by Made Beside. This policy does not claim that the browser, hosting platform or other websites never use cookies.

Why there is no optional-cookie banner

No optional tracking is configured in the site application, so it does not show an accept-all advertising or analytics banner. If optional tracking or third-party embeds are introduced, their purposes, providers and consent requirements must be reviewed before they are enabled. Where prior consent is required, those technologies must stay off until you choose them.

Your controls

Your browser can restrict or clear cookies. Restricting necessary provider cookies may affect sign-in or access. Questions about this website can be sent to hello@madebeside.com. See the privacy policy for inquiry handling.
