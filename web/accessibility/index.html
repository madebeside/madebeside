Accessibility

The story should be available to everyone.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Using this website

The site is designed with semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, labelled fields and descriptive errors. The main information is available as text; decorative visual effects are hidden from assistive technology. You do not need to drag objects or interact with an animation to read the services.

Motion and reading

Use “Pause motion” at the bottom of the screen to stop decorative movement. The website also responds to your device’s reduced-motion preference. Service descriptions remain readable when motion is paused. A basic agency introduction and email contact are available when JavaScript is disabled. No audio plays automatically.

Contact and alternatives

If something is difficult to use, email hello@madebeside.com. Tell us which page or task caused difficulty and your preferred way to receive the information. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis. An accessible alternative can be discussed with you.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing responsibility. These features are not a claim of independent certification or perfect compatibility with every browser and assistive technology. Reports of barriers help guide corrections.
